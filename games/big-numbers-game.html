How Big Is a Million, Billion & Trillion?

They all just sound like "a lot." Play these mini-games to feel the difference.

See how the million bar is basically invisible? That's the whole point — a trillion is to a billion what a billion is to a million. A billion is way closer to zero than it is to a trillion.

Mind-blower starter pack

⏱️ 1 million seconds ≈ 11.5 days ago.
📅 1 billion seconds ≈ 31.7 years ago — back in the early 1990s.
🦣 1 trillion seconds ≈ 31,700 years ago — humans were sharing Earth with woolly mammoths and Neanderthals had just died out.

🎂 The "if you lived that long" test

A typical human life is about 80 years ≈ 2.5 billion seconds.

You'll live roughly 2.5 billion seconds — so you blow past a million seconds in your first 2 weeks, and cross a billion seconds around your 32nd birthday. 🎉
To reach a trillion seconds you'd need to live ~400 lifetimes back to back. Nobody's getting there.
